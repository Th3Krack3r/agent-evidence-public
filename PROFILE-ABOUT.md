### About

I build the operational layer around AI agent runtimes — specifically the SIEM and Helm packaging that lets security teams actually adopt runtime enforcement systems.

Primary work: **agent-evidence-bridge** and **warrantkit-siem-bridge** (companion projects to Daniel Kissel’s WarrantKit). These turn agent evidence into normalized, SIEM-ready events (Splunk HEC, Datadog, webhooks) while preserving the critical trust boundary: evidence flows out, authority stays with the runtime.

Background spans Windows registry forensics, Android partition/EDL/fastboot internals, vehicle electronics diagnostics, and shipping practical open-core infrastructure under West Electronics LLC.
